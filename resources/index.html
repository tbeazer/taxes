Get free help preparing your return

Free preparation and filing options run by or through the IRS.

VITA and TCEFree tax return preparation

IRS-certified volunteers prepare and e-file returns for free. VITA serves people with low to moderate income, people with disabilities, and people with limited English. TCE focuses on people 60 and older. Find a site with the IRS locator or by calling 800-906-9887.

What to bringChecklist for free tax return preparation

The IRS list of documents to bring to a VITA or TCE site: photo ID, Social Security cards, every W-2 and 1099, last year's return, bank account numbers, child care provider information.

Publication 3676-BServices provided by VITA/TCE (English and Spanish)

What volunteers can and can't prepare, so you know before you go.

IRS Free FileFree online software

Free brand-name tax software for people under an income limit, plus fillable forms anyone can use.

MilTaxMilitary OneSource

Free tax software and expert help for active-duty service members, eligible family members, survivors, and veterans up to 365 days after separation or retirement.

Publication 4134Low Income Taxpayer Clinic List

Free or low-cost help with an IRS dispute or audit for people with low income.

The intake sheet

The form VITA and TCE sites ask every taxpayer to fill out first. It works as a checklist for anyone getting ready to file.

Form 13614-CIntake/Interview & Quality Review Sheet (Rev. 10-2026)

Page 1: you, your spouse, your marital status, your household, and how you want your refund. Page 2: income. Page 3: expenses and tax-related events, such as selling a home, an HSA, Marketplace coverage, a new vehicle, cancelled debt, or a credit disallowed before. Page 4: optional questions. Each section of our Questions page lists the matching page.

Form 15080Consent to Disclose Tax Return Information to VITA/TCE Sites

Optional consent, included with Form 13614-C, that lets a volunteer site's software carry your information forward to next year.

Form 13614-NRNonresident Alien Intake and Interview Sheet

The intake sheet for international students, scholars and other nonresident aliens.

Publication 4885Screening Sheet for Health Savings Accounts

The extra screening sheet volunteers use when a taxpayer has an HSA.
