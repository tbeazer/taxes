# Free Help & Resources

Where new filers can get free help, and the forms and guides that IRS-certified VITA (Volunteer Income Tax Assistance) and TCE (Tax Counseling for the Elderly) volunteers use. Everything here is published free by the IRS or another federal agency.

Browse it as a page: [resources/index.html](index.html).

## New to taxes? Start here

Plain-language IRS guides and tools for first-time filers.

- **[Identity Protection PIN](https://www.irs.gov/identity-theft-fraud-scams/get-an-identity-protection-pin)**: IRS.gov. A free six-digit PIN that stops anyone else from filing a return with your Social Security number.
- **[Understanding your IRS notice or letter](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/understanding-your-irs-notice-or-letter)**: IRS.gov. Look up a notice by its number to see what it means and what to do.
- **[Publication 17](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p17.pdf)**: Your Federal Income Tax. The IRS's main guide to individual income tax, updated every year. Start here when a question comes up.
- **[Interactive Tax Assistant](https://www.irs.gov/help/ita)**: IRS.gov tool. Answer a few questions to find out your filing status, whether someone is your dependent, or whether you qualify for a credit.
- **[Tax Topics](https://www.irs.gov/help/tax-topics)**: IRS.gov. Short IRS explanations of common subjects, from tips to medical expenses.
- **[Publication 501](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p501.pdf)**: Dependents, Standard Deduction, and Filing Information. Who has to file, which filing status to use, and who you can claim.
- **[Tax Withholding Estimator](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator)**: IRS.gov tool. Check whether your paycheck withholding is on track, then update your W-4.
- **[IRS Online Account](https://www.irs.gov/your-account)**: IRS.gov. See what you owe, your payments, and records from past returns.
- **[Get Transcript](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/get-transcript)**: IRS.gov. Download a summary of a past return or of the W-2 and 1099 information the IRS has on file.
- **[Publication 1](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1.pdf)**: Your Rights as a Taxpayer. The Taxpayer Bill of Rights in plain language.

## Get free help preparing your return

Free preparation and filing options run by or through the IRS.

- **[VITA and TCE](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/free-tax-return-preparation-for-qualifying-taxpayers)**: Free tax return preparation. IRS-certified volunteers prepare and e-file returns for free. VITA serves people with low to moderate income, people with disabilities, and people with limited English. TCE focuses on people 60 and older. Find a site with the IRS locator or by calling 800-906-9887.
- **[What to bring](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/checklist-for-free-tax-return-preparation)**: Checklist for free tax return preparation. The IRS list of documents to bring to a VITA or TCE site: photo ID, Social Security cards, every W-2 and 1099, last year's return, bank account numbers, child care provider information.
- **[Publication 3676-B](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p3676bsp.pdf)**: Services provided by VITA/TCE (English and Spanish). What volunteers can and can't prepare, so you know before you go.
- **[IRS Free File](https://www.irs.gov/e-file-do-your-taxes-for-free)**: Free online software. Free brand-name tax software for people under an income limit, plus fillable forms anyone can use.
- **[MilTax](https://www.militaryonesource.mil/financial-legal/taxes/miltax-military-tax-services/)**: Military OneSource. Free tax software and expert help for active-duty service members, eligible family members, survivors, and veterans up to 365 days after separation or retirement.
- **[Publication 4134](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4134.pdf)**: Low Income Taxpayer Clinic List. Free or low-cost help with an IRS dispute or audit for people with low income.

## The intake sheet

The form VITA and TCE sites ask every taxpayer to fill out first. It works as a checklist for anyone getting ready to file.

- **[Form 13614-C](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f13614c.pdf)**: Intake/Interview & Quality Review Sheet (Rev. 10-2026). Page 1: you, your spouse, your marital status, your household, and how you want your refund. Page 2: income. Page 3: expenses and tax-related events, such as selling a home, an HSA, Marketplace coverage, a new vehicle, cancelled debt, or a credit disallowed before. Page 4: optional questions. Each section of our Questions page lists the matching page.
- **[Form 15080](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f15080.pdf)**: Consent to Disclose Tax Return Information to VITA/TCE Sites. Optional consent, included with Form 13614-C, that lets a volunteer site's software carry your information forward to next year.
- **[Form 13614-NR](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f13614nr.pdf)**: Nonresident Alien Intake and Interview Sheet. The intake sheet for international students, scholars and other nonresident aliens.
- **[Publication 4885](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4885.pdf)**: Screening Sheet for Health Savings Accounts. The extra screening sheet volunteers use when a taxpayer has an HSA.

## What VITA and TCE volunteers use

The IRS's own training and reference materials for volunteers, all free and public.

- **[Publication 4012](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4012.pdf)**: VITA/TCE Volunteer Resource Guide. The volunteers' quick-reference binder: filing status and dependent charts, income and credit decision trees, and software tips.
- **[Publication 4491](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4491.pdf)**: VITA/TCE Training Guide. The full training course, lesson by lesson, with examples.
- **[Publication 4491-X](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4491x.pdf)**: VITA/TCE Training Supplement. Late-season updates to the training guide for the current filing season.
- **[Link & Learn Taxes](https://apps.irs.gov/app/vita/)**: Free online volunteer training. The IRS's online course for VITA and TCE volunteers. Anyone can use it to learn to prepare a basic return.
- **[Publication 5101](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5101.pdf)**: Intake/Interview and Quality Review Training. How to interview a taxpayer and review a return for accuracy.
- **[Publication 5838](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5838.pdf)**: Intake/Interview and Quality Review Handbook. The companion handbook to the intake and quality review process.
- **[Publication 4961](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4961.pdf)**: Volunteer Standards of Conduct: Ethics Training. The ethics training every volunteer completes.
- **[Form 13615](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f13615.pdf)**: Volunteer Standards of Conduct Agreement. The agreement every volunteer signs.
- **[Publication 4299](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4299.pdf)**: Privacy, Confidentiality and Civil Rights. How volunteer sites protect taxpayer information.
- **[Form 6744](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f6744.pdf)**: VITA/TCE Volunteer Assistor's Test/Retest. The certification test, with practice scenarios that make good study material.
- **[Publication 5166](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5166.pdf)**: Volunteer Quality Site Requirements. The quality standards every VITA and TCE site follows.
- **[Publication 4011](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4011.pdf)**: Foreign Student and Scholar Volunteer Resource Guide. The resource guide for preparing nonresident returns.
