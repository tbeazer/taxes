# Form 1040 — Tax Year 2026

Interactive flow map for tax year 2026 (returns filed in 2027). Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser.

> **Draft year.** The 2026 dollar amounts are final IRS figures (Rev. Proc. 2025-32, IR-2025-111 and Notice 2025-67). The 2026 Form 1040 and many schedules are still IRS drafts, so line numbers and new forms such as Schedule 3-A can change before the final versions are released.

For each form or schedule, the map shows the Form 1040 line it flows to, what it's for, its 2026 limits (highlighted), due diligence interview questions, and links to the official IRS form and instructions. Form links open the IRS's 2026 draft where one is posted, the 2026 version where it is already final, and otherwise the current version, which may still be the 2025 form.

## What changes for 2026

- Standard deduction $16,100 / $32,200 / $24,150.
- New line 12f: up to $1,000 ($2,000 joint) of cash gifts to charity for people who don't itemize. Itemized charitable gifts count only above 0.5% of AGI.
- Line order changes: Schedule 1-A deductions move to line 13a and the QBI deduction to line 13b.
- Child and dependent care credit rate rises to as much as 50%; dependent care benefit exclusion rises to $7,500.
- Gambling losses limited to 90% of losses; mortgage insurance premiums deductible again.
- Repayment caps on excess advance premium tax credit removed.
- New Schedule 3-A (Federal Public Benefit) for refundable credits (line 32b).
- Residential energy and clean vehicle credits are gone, except carryforwards and vehicles acquired by September 30, 2025.
- Trump Accounts for children: contributions began July 4, 2026.

## About the due diligence questions

The official due diligence questions are on Form 8867 and in its instructions. The interview questions on every card are an original study aid written for this project, not official IRS text. The paid preparer penalty is $665 per failure for returns filed in 2027.
