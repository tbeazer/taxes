What changes for 2026

  • Standard deduction $16,100 / $32,200 / $24,150.
  • New line 12f: up to $1,000 ($2,000 joint) of cash gifts to charity for people who don't itemize. Itemized charitable gifts count only above 0.5% of AGI.
  • Line order changes: Schedule 1-A deductions move to line 13a and the QBI deduction to line 13b.
  • Child and dependent care credit rate rises to as much as 50%; dependent care benefit exclusion rises to $7,500.
  • Gambling losses limited to 90% of losses; mortgage insurance premiums deductible again.
  • Repayment caps on excess advance premium tax credit removed.
  • New Schedule 3-A (Federal Public Benefit) for refundable credits (line 32b).
  • Residential energy and clean vehicle credits are gone, except carryforwards and vehicles acquired by September 30, 2025.
  • Trump Accounts for children: contributions began July 4, 2026.
