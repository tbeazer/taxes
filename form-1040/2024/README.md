# Form 1040 — Tax Year 2024

Interactive flow map for tax year 2024 (returns filed in 2025). Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser.

For each form or schedule, the map shows the Form 1040 line it flows to, what it's for, its 2024 limits (highlighted), due diligence interview questions, and links to the official IRS form and instructions. Form links open the 2024 versions in the IRS prior-year archive; a few forms the IRS revises only occasionally link to the current version.

## How 2024 differs from 2025

- No Schedule 1-A: the tips, overtime, car loan interest and senior deductions start in 2025.
- SALT deduction capped at $10,000 ($5,000 married filing separately).
- Child tax credit is $2,000 per child, with up to $1,700 refundable.
- Clean vehicle buyers could transfer the credit to the dealer at the time of sale.
- Residential clean energy and home improvement credits available.
- The standard deduction is on line 12 and the QBI deduction on line 13.

## About the due diligence questions

The official due diligence questions are on Form 8867 and in its instructions. The interview questions on every card are an original study aid written for this project, not official IRS text. The paid preparer penalty is $635 per failure for returns filed in 2025.
