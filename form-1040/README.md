# Form 1040

Form 1040, *U.S. Individual Income Tax Return*, is the main federal income tax return for individuals. Form 1040-SR is the same return in a larger-print version for people 65 and older.

The return works top to bottom: income, minus adjustments, gives adjusted gross income (AGI); minus deductions, gives taxable income; tax on that, minus credits, plus other taxes, gives total tax; payments and refundable credits are compared with total tax to get the refund or balance due.
