# Form 1040 — Tax Year 2025

Interactive flow map for tax year 2025 (returns filed in 2026). Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser.

For each form or schedule, the map shows:

- the Form 1040 line (or schedule line) it flows to
- what it's for
- its 2025 dollar limits, percentages and thresholds, highlighted
- due diligence interview questions, with links to the official IRS due diligence resources for the items covered by Form 8867
- links to the official IRS form, instructions and publications

A table at the bottom collects every 2025 limit in one place.

## What changed for 2025

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (Public Law 119-21) changed several 2025 amounts: a higher standard deduction, a $2,200 child tax credit, a $40,000 SALT cap, new Schedule 1-A deductions (qualified tips, qualified overtime, car loan interest, and the senior deduction), and the end of the residential energy credits after 2025. Form 1040 also gained line 13b for the Schedule 1-A deductions, and the standard deduction moved to line 12e.

## About the due diligence questions

For the earned income credit, child tax credit/additional child tax credit/credit for other dependents, American opportunity credit and head of household status, the official due diligence questions are on [Form 8867](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8867.pdf) and in its [instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8867.pdf). The interview questions on every card are an original study aid written for this project, not official IRS text.
