How a Form 1040 flows

  1. Lines 1–9Income

    Wages, interest, dividends, retirement income, capital gains, Schedule 1 income.

  2. Lines 10–11Adjustments

    Above-the-line items subtracted to reach adjusted gross income (AGI).

  3. Lines 12–15Deductions

    Standard or itemized deduction, then other deductions, to reach taxable income.

  4. Lines 16–18Tax

    Tax from the Tax Table, plus additional taxes from Schedule 2.

  5. Lines 19–24Credits

    Nonrefundable credits reduce the tax; other taxes are added for total tax.

  6. Lines 25–33Payments

    Withholding, estimated payments and refundable credits.

  7. Lines 34–38Result

    Refund if payments are more than total tax; balance due if less.
